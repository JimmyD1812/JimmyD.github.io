""Despite my noble birth, I do not place myself above other folk... though between you and me, they are clearly all beneath me.""

Retired Knight

Reddyl

Paladin 5; Oath of Vengeance

Configure custom character art URL below:

Lost Mine of Phandelver DM: Jeremy

Despite his noble dragonborn birth and golden scales, Reddyl suffered from a fascinating double nature: publicly declaring that all commoners have the same blood and dedicating his steel to protect those 'beneath' him, while privately holding a firm, quiet belief that absolutely everyone else was beneath him. Guided by this strict sense of noble obligation, he set out to find fame and protect the frontier from chaotic forces.

Campaign Conclusion:

Reddyl's adventure came to a glorious and complete end exactly as written in the annals of Phandalin. Alongside his steadfast companions, including the wizard Aelin Whitethorn, they braved the dark depths of Wave Echo Cave, vanquished the Black Spider's forces, and successfully cleared the lost mine. Having restored the legendary Forge of Spells, Reddyl secured his place as a hero of the common people, finally retiring in absolute triumph.
