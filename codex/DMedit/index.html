DM Desk

Not linked from the player site. Keep this address to yourself.

How saving works: edits are stored in this browser as you work. When you're ready to go live, save a GitHub token in the Publish box below (one time), then hit Publish to jimmyd.space — it commits both data files and starts the Rebuild website (no cards job) rebuild for you. The player data file only ever contains player-visible articles — DM-only and draft articles are written only to dm-data.js, which lives in this folder and is never loaded by the player page.

Article templates

Templates travel in dm-data.js when you Publish, so both your devices share the set. "Save as template" in the article form captures an article's headings and structure only, never its words.

Snapshots & rollback

Full copies of everything in this Desk, kept in this browser under their own key, so they survive "Discard local edits" and never get published. One is taken automatically before each Publish and on the first open of each day. Restoring replaces what's in the Desk now, and a safety snapshot is taken first, so a rollback can itself be undone.
